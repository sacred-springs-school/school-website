Enrollment Procedure

Follow these simple steps to enroll your child at Sacred Springs School.

School Tour

If you would like to learn more about Sacred Springs School, schedule a tour with the school office to explore our facilities, receive key information, and ask any questions you may have.

Registration & Fee Payment

Once you have chosen Sacred Springs School, payment of the registration, enrollment, and term fees is required to secure your child’s place.

Proof of payment should be submitted via email or at the school office, and a receipt will be issued upon request.

Enrollment & Start

Your child may begin immediately if space is available or will be placed on a waiting list.

Required documents:

  • Completed Enrollment Form
  • Medical Consent Form
  • Photography Release Form
  • Copy of Immunization Record

Documents can be submitted at the school office or via email.
